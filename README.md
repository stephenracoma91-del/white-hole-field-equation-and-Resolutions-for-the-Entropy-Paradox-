$$\mathcal{E}_{WH}(\rho) = \left[ \frac{k_B \cdot \ln(\Omega_0)}{\Delta\tau \cdot c^{D-4}} \right] \cdot \left[ G\rho\tau^2 \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{\rho}{\rho_{\text{crit}}} \right) \right]$$

where each term is defined:
* $\mathcal{E}_{WH}(\rho)$: Energy of the white hole as a function of density
* $k_B$: Boltzmann constant
* $\Omega_0$: Initial condition constant
* $\Delta\tau$: Time interval
* $c$: Speed of light
* $G$: Gravitational constant
* $\rho$: Current density of collapsing matter
* $\rho_{\text{crit}}$: Critical density
* $\tau$: Time parameter
